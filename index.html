?How to use
  1. Choose a calculator from the dropdown at the top.
  2. Enter the deal on the Calculator tab: the loan amount, the subvention %, the target IRR % and the broken-period days. Tap the small buttons for quick values.
  3. Pick a tenure from the row of buttons (3 to 84 months). The big card shows that tenure's EMI, customer rate, total repayment and interest.
  4. Matrix shows every tenure in one table, and the full payment break-up below it. Tap a row to select that tenure. If there is a broken period, payment 1 is the broken-period interest.
  5. Quote (the round button) makes a customer-ready quote. Type a phone number to instantly WhatsApp it, or copy/share it as text or an image.
  6. Compare puts two tenures side by side. The budget box finds the tenures that fit the EMI a customer can pay.
iWhat the boxes mean
  1. Loan amount: the asset or loan amount including GST.
  2. Subvention: the % of the loan received from the brand or dealer, including GST. The card also shows it without GST.
  3. Target IRR: the yearly return you want. The calculator finds the customer interest rate that reaches it for each tenure.
  4. Broken-period days: the days between the loan start and the first EMI date. The interest for these days is charged as payment 1.
  5. Net initial outflow: the loan amount minus the subvention received without GST.
✓Good to know
  1. Subvention and days can be left blank, which counts as 0. With both at 0, the customer rate equals your target IRR.
  2. The customer rate is rounded to 2 decimals before the EMI is worked out, the same way the EMI Store site does it.
  3. The customer rate never goes below 0%. If your target is already reached at 0%, the rate stays at 0%.
  4. Saved quotes stay on this phone. Use the ☾ and ☀ buttons at the top to switch between night and day colours.

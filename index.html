How the workbook model is applied
  1. Subvention excluding GST = amount × (subvention % ÷ 118%).
  2. Net initial outflow to NJC = amount − net subvention receipt. Brokerage is ₹0 because the workbook's brokerage cells are blank.
  3. EMI uses the PMT-equivalent reducing-balance formula with customer annual rate ÷ 12.
  4. Broken period = −(amount × customer rate ÷ 100 ÷ 360) × days. It counts as a cash-flow period only when the customer rate is above 0% and the days are above 0.
  5. Achieved IRR uses the initial outflow, the broken-period flow when it applies, and the EMI flows, then annualises monthly IRR × 12.
  6. If the target is already met at a 0% customer rate, the rate stays at 0% and the row is flagged. The rate never goes negative.

How the workbook model is applied

1. Subvention excluding GST = amount × (subvention percentage ÷ 118%).
2. Net initial outflow to NJC = asset amount − net subvention receipt. Brokerage is set to zero because the uploaded workbook's brokerage cells are blank.
3. EMI uses Excel's PMT-equivalent reducing-balance formula, based on customer annual rate ÷ 12 and the selected tenure.
4. Broken period = −(asset amount × customer annual rate ÷ 100 ÷ 360) × days. It is included as a cash-flow period only when the customer rate is above 0%, matching Excel's blank-cell behaviour when rate is zero.
5. Achieved IRR is calculated using the initial NJC outflow, the broken-period cash flow when applicable, and the EMI cash flows, then annualized as monthly IRR × 12.
6. If the target IRR is already met or exceeded at a 0% customer rate, the calculator keeps the rate at 0% and flags the target result instead of using a negative interest rate.
